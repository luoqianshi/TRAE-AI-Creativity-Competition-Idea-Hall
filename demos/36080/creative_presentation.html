不仅仅是工具，更是“活”的思考空间 Not just a tool, a "living" thinking space

LinkWeave 是一个深度整合了多模态 AI 交互、工作流自动化与知识管理的智能编辑平台。它将静态的文档阅读和写作过程，转变为与智能体双向互动的沉浸式体验。 LinkWeave is an intelligent editing platform deeply integrating multimodal AI, workflow automation, and knowledge management. It transforms static reading and writing into an immersive, two-way interactive experience.

高度模块化与可扩展性 Highly Modular & Extensible

支持多文档并行处理、自定义大模型 API 接入、复杂工作流编排以及 MCP（模型上下文协议）集成。它像一个系统级的神经中枢，链接一切你所需的生产力组件。 Supports parallel multi-document processing, custom LLM APIs, complex workflow orchestration, and MCP integration. It acts as a system-level neural hub linking all your productivity components.

这个产品的目的是什么What is the purpose?

1. 打破割裂的工具链孤岛 1. Breaking Fragmented Toolchains

传统的学习与工作流极度分散：用 A 软件看 PDF，在 B 网页查资料问 AI，再回 C 软件做笔记。这种频繁的上下文切换（Context Switching Tax）严重消耗专注力。LinkWeave 实现了“All-in-One”的沉浸态。 Traditional workflows are heavily fragmented: reading PDFs in App A, asking AI in Web B, taking notes in App C. This context switching tax destroys focus. LinkWeave achieves an "All-in-One" immersive state.

2. 消除机械被动的 AI 交互 2. Eliminating Passive AI Interaction

当下的 AI 多为“一问一答”的冷冰冰机器，它在等待你的指令。我们解决了 AI 缺乏“主动性”的问题。在 LinkWeave 中，AI 可以介入你的思维过程，当你思路卡壳时，它会像真人导师一样主动打断、启发你。 Current AI is mostly a cold "Q&A" machine waiting for commands. We solve this lack of "proactivity". In LinkWeave, AI intervenes in your thinking process; when you're stuck, it interrupts and inspires you like a human tutor.

这个产品的价值是什么？What is its value?

极致的个性化：编织你的专属工具 Ultimate Personalization: Weave Your Tool

你不是在被动地适应一个软件，而是在主动地“编织”它。无论是调用不同的底层大模型 API，还是拖拽配置复杂的工作流逻辑，LinkWeave 赋予了你定义生产力工具的最高自由度。 You aren't passively adapting to software; you are actively "weaving" it. Whether calling different LLM APIs or dragging/dropping complex workflow logic, LinkWeave gives you the ultimate freedom to define your productivity tool.

有温度的数字孪生伴侣 A Warm Digital Twin Companion

它的核心价值在于“成长性”与“共情”。你的每一次交互、每一道错题，都会被沉淀到私人库中。时间越久，它越懂你的思考盲区，最终成为一个具备长时记忆、专属你一人的 AI 一对一导师。 Its core value lies in "growth" and "empathy". Every interaction and mistake is stored in your private base. Over time, it understands your blind spots, ultimately becoming a 1-on-1 AI tutor with long-term memory exclusively for you.
