Motivation

When exploring complex numbers, each added imaginary unit grants a new degree of freedom — yet the third breaks algebraic closure. Quaternions resolve this beautifully.

To a physics student, math should be seen. This tool visualizes how a vector spins and precesses under quaternion rotation, and reveals on the side planes that two flips along different axes cannot be merged into one — the non-commutativity that faithfully mirrors real 3D space, and why quaternions are essential in robotics & attitude dynamics.
