Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a custom piece take?

The frontend demo uses a 7 to 14 day lead time. The final production window should be confirmed after WordPress/WooCommerce and fulfillment rules are set.

Is the preview exact?

No. The preview estimates the bead map and color count. A handmade piece can vary slightly, and the design should be confirmed before production.

Can I upload any image?

The custom flow should accept common image formats for preview. The final store needs a clear rule for copyrighted characters, offensive content, and images that cannot translate well into beads.

Is checkout real right now?

No. Checkout is a demo until the frontend is approved and WordPress/WooCommerce is connected. This is a showcase artifact.
