📦 IAP 协议帧格式
+------+------+------+----------+------+-------+--------+
| HEAD | LEN | CMD | ADDR | DATA | CRC | TAIL |
+------+------+------+----------+------+-------+--------+
| 2B | 2B | 1B | 4B | N | 2B | 2B |
+------+------+------+----------+------+-------+--------+
● HEAD: 0x55AA (帧头)
● LEN: 数据长度
● CMD: 命令码
● ADDR: Flash地址
● DATA: 数据域
● CRC: CRC16校验
● TAIL: 0xAA55 (帧尾)
