声明式编排 / Declarative Engine

仅需寥寥数行 YAML，即可在本地唤醒暴风雨般的并发处理能力，将业务逻辑与底层通信彻底解耦。

forgeflow.yaml 
# 定义一个高并发本地大模型数据清洗工作流
name: "Data-Refactor-Flow"
engine: "rust-core-v1"
concurrency: 10000

pipeline:
  - step: "read_files"
    path: "./raw_data/**/*.json"
  - step: "llm_process"
    model: "local-llama-3-8b-q4"
    prompt: "Extract key metrics and format as strict JSON."
  - step: "write_output"
    dest: "./clean_data/"
$ forgeflow --init success
