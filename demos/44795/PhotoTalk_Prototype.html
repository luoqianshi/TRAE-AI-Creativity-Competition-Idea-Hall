你的表达
Today I go to a coffee shop. I find a window seat. The barista is very nice. I order a latte and a pastry. The coffee has good smell. It has many people. I want come here again.
修正建议
语法错误
I go
I went
描述过去发生的事应使用一般过去时，"go" 的过去式是 "went"。
表达优化
has good smell
the aroma of fresh coffee was amazing
"aroma" 是描述香气的更地道词汇，比 "good smell" 更精准、更高级。
表达优化
It has many people
the cozy atmosphere was perfect
"cozy atmosphere" 是描述咖啡馆氛围的经典搭配，比单纯说人多更有画面感。
语法错误
I want come
I want to come
"want" 后面接动词时，需要使用 "want to + 动词原形" 的结构。
优化后的版本
Today I went to a coffee shop. I found a window seat by the counter. The barista was very friendly. I ordered a latte and a pastry. The aroma of fresh coffee was amazing. The cozy atmosphere made me feel relaxed. I'd love to come here again.
