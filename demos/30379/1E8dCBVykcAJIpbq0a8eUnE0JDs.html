Why Systematic Debugging

The Developer's Daily Dilemma

Developers spend an average of 1-3 hours daily on non-functional bug investigation. Without a structured approach, time is wasted and problems can recur -- especially cross-layer issues involving browser compatibility, framework regressions, and IME interactions.

1

Random Trial & Error

Changing things randomly, relying on luck. Hours pass with no progress.

2

Poor AI Usage

Pasting error messages directly to AI with "how to fix" -- getting generic, unactionable answers.

3

Low-Quality Reports

Issues missing browser version, reproduction steps, or logs. Maintainers cannot respond.

4

Non-Reusable Experience

Debugging process scattered across chat logs and memory. Starting from scratch next time.

5

Hard Root Cause Location

Browser + framework + dependencies layered on top of each other. Who is responsible?

6

Team Time Waste

10 developers x 1 extra hour/day = ~2500 hours/year of lost productivity.
