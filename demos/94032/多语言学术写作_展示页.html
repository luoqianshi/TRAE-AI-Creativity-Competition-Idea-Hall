📊 质量对比

通用翻译 vs 学术AI翻译

同样是机器翻译，懂学术的AI和不学术的AI，输出质量天壤之别。

❌ 通用机器翻译
This study uses deep learning algorithm to classify medical image data, experimental results show that the proposed method is obviously better than traditional machine learning algorithm, reached 94.7% classification accuracy.

In addition, through explainable analysis of the model, we find convolutional neural network has strong feature extraction ability when identifying lesion areas.

⚠ 问题：术语不精确（"obviously better than"非学术表达）、语法错误（"algorithm"未复数）、中式英语痕迹重、缺乏学术连接词
✓ 学术AI翻译
This study employs deep learning algorithms to classify medical imaging data. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method significantly outperforms conventional machine learning approaches, achieving a classification accuracy of 94.7%.

Furthermore, through explainability analysis of the model, we observe that convolutional neural networks exhibit robust feature extraction capabilities in identifying lesion regions.

✓ 优化：术语精确（"significantly outperforms"）、语法规范（复数+从句）、学术词汇丰富（employs/demonstrate/exhibit）、句式多样化
