Deep Learning for Image Recognition: A Comprehensive Survey

John Smith, Emily Chen, David Liu  |  IEEE Trans. on Pattern Analysis 2025 📖 被引 234 次
This paper presents a comprehensive survey of deep learning techniques for image recognition. We systematically review the evolution from early neural network approaches to state-of-the-art transformer-based models. Key topics include architecture innovations (ResNet, EfficientNet, Vision Transformers), training strategies (self-supervised learning, data augmentation), and applications in medical imaging, autonomous driving, and remote sensing. We also discuss computational efficiency and model deployment challenges.
